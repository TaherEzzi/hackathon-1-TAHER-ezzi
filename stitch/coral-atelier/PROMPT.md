# Stitch prompts — Coral Atelier (light)

Open [Google Stitch](https://stitch.withgoogle.com). Create a project named **A Una Milla — Coral Atelier**. Set the device to **Web, desktop**.

Upload `DESIGN.md` in this folder as the project design system before generating screens. The prompts below describe layout and words only. The design file owns color, type, and corner radius. If Stitch has no design-system upload in the box you are using, paste `DESIGN.md` into the project first, then paste one screen prompt.

Attach screenshots with the prompt’s image button. Crop the homepage only. Do not attach their logos for tracing.

- https://www.aesop.com — warm paper, hairlines, quiet type
- https://www.readcereal.com — travel editorial with room around the words
- https://www.hermes.com — one orange mark, used once
- https://www.belmond.com — a journey, told formally

Generate **one screen per paste**. Desktop first. After each screen looks right, generate the phone version of that same screen with the edit prompt at the bottom.

## Screen 1 — Home

```text
A desktop marketing homepage for A Una Milla, a service that tells a traveller the euro saving on a flight if they use miles they can already earn. English. One screen, 1440 wide. The page is a poster, not a brochure.

PLATFORM: Web, desktop-first.

PAGE STRUCTURE:
1. Header: left, a small armillary ring mark (two thin circles, one tilted ellipse, a short tick at twelve o’clock, one bead on the orbit) beside the wordmark “A Una Milla” and a second line “of your destination.” Right: three text links, Home, Brand, Thread. Home is current. No filled bar, no shadow.
2. Hero, split: left column is an uppercase eyebrow “A mile from your destination,” then the headline “The saving is the trip.” set on two lines, then the figure €1,890 as the largest type on the page with the small label “sample saving,” then one sentence: “The same seat to Johannesburg. Much less money. The house reads the cash fare, the Avios, and the taxes, and leaves the saving in euros where you cannot miss it.” Then two square buttons: “Open the thread” and “See the brand.”
3. Right column: the armillary mile-ring, large, machined metal, slow and still, not a globe and not an airplane. Under it, a mono caption “MAD” a hairline “JNB” and “Business · 90 days.”
4. Ledger under the hero, three columns divided by hairlines, not cards: Cash €3,840 / Award 62,500 Avios with “+ €214 taxes” / Verdict “Use points.”
5. One line of small type: “Brand sample. These figures are not a live fare.”
6. Footer: the wordmark and “Sample figures.” No link farm, no newsletter, no social icons.

Do not add a feature grid, icons, testimonials, airplane photography, a map, a gradient, or a second accent color. Leave most of the page empty.
```

## Screen 2 — Thread

```text
A desktop product screen for A Una Milla. Same header as the home page. This is the working page: a conversation that ends in one verdict card. English. 1440 wide. The column is narrow, about 740px, left-aligned inside the page frame. It is not a phone messenger stretched to desktop.

PLATFORM: Web, desktop-first.

PAGE STRUCTURE:
1. Header: identical to the home page. Thread is the current link.
2. Eyebrow: “Sample thread.” Headline: “Madrid → Johannesburg.” Subline: “Business · 90 days · one adult. No account.”
3. Two square toggle buttons: “Plan the next 90 days” (pressed) and “Book now.”
4. A guest message, right aligned, hairline box, no avatar, no tail: “I want Madrid to Johannesburg in business within 90 days. I hold Iberia Avios and I spend on Revolut Metal.”
5. A house reply, left aligned, no avatar: “Ninety days. On the spending you already do, the Avios gap can close before the flight. If the seat exists, the award drops the cash fare by this much.”
6. A verdict plaque, full column width. Small label “90-day runway” and a badge “estimate.” The largest type in the plaque is €1,890 with the caption “saved if the award seat opens.” Then hairline rows: Target 62,500 Iberia Avios / Already held 32,000 Avios / Gap 30,500 Avios / Cash for the seat €3,840 / Award taxes €214. A closing line with the mile tick: “Use points.”
7. Composer: a square text field with placeholder “Describe a trip. You will only see the sample.” and a square button “Send.”

Do not use chat bubbles with tails, avatars, emoji, a sidebar, or a dashboard of charts.
```

## Screen 3 — Logo sheet

```text
A single brand sheet for A Una Milla on the page color, 1440 wide, mostly empty. This screen is the logo asset. Center the armillary mark: two thin circles, one tilted ellipse, a tick at twelve o’clock, one small bead on the right of the orbit. The mark is drawn in metal line, not filled, about 280px. Under it, the wordmark “A Una Milla” in the display face, and “of your destination” in the body face on the next line. In the bottom left, a 64px favicon crop of the ring and tick only, labeled “Favicon.” In the bottom right, the same lockup reversed on a deep-blue rectangle labeled “On ink,” still using the coral tick. Show clear space as a faint square around the mark equal to the height of the tick. No slogan, no mockup, no stationery scene, no airplane.
```
